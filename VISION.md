# TasteTable

TasteTable helps a caregiver plan meals and outings based on someone's tastes and care constraints, then carry the original reasons, conversations with venues and visit outcomes into the next decision. The useful result is a workable week with current context, rather than a pile of unrelated recommendations and saved files.

## What good looks like

The itinerary, dated visit and editable reply should stay together at phone size. Original Qloo taste signals and heuristic constraint checks must remain distinguishable from later human-entered venue replies. A complete private save and reopen cannot silently lose that distinction. Less-used exports, backup controls and raw provenance should not push a visit decision out of reach.

Compare with [Wanderlog's itinerary interface](https://wanderlog.com/) for day navigation and connected planning, while keeping TasteTable's distinctive care and venue-conversation context.

## Working product

The [current runnable TasteTable release](https://github.com/Jacob-Met/tastetable/releases/tag/v0.3.11-visit-outcomes) brings the seven-day itinerary, immediate complete save and dated note editor into one phone workflow, with file administration opened on demand heuristic care checks labeled at the visit itself, and imported attendance outcomes beside matching future venues. Its Linux package uses fictional Qloo fixtures and a scripted model; it is not evidence for real venue recommendations or care suitability.

The live faults and current build are maintained in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md). Start with its highest actionable fault, and change the delivered product instead of adding another parallel interface.
